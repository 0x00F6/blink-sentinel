Your Blink system, armed by presence.

Blink Sentinel watches your selected home Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth device and automatically controls your Blink system after a confirmed departure or return. No manual routine. No connection to the monitored Wi-Fi required.

01 // capabilities

Built for quiet, local presence automation.

Terminal-inspired UI, explicit state, actionable diagnostics and Android-native background monitoring.

[01] WIFI

Wi-Fi presence

Watch an exact home SSID without requiring the phone to connect to that network.

[02] BLE

Bluetooth mode

Use a compatible BLE device as the home presence signal on Android 12 and later.

[03] BLINK

Automatic control

Arm after confirmed absence and optionally disarm when the selected home sensor returns.

[04] STATE

Conservative decisions

Fresh radio evidence and configurable delays reduce accidental state changes from stale observations.

[05] LOG

Detailed diagnostics

Readable event history, red error states and copyable technical details when something goes wrong.

[06] ANDROID

Native experience

Kotlin + Jetpack Compose, persistent settings and an interface designed to match Android background constraints.
